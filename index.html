Our Story

A decade of turning special moments into cherished memories

Founded by Leandie O’Brien, Creations began in Wellington with a simple belief: every celebration deserves to feel extraordinary. For more than ten years we have partnered with families, couples, schools and businesses across the Cape Winelands to design events that are elegant, personal and effortlessly memorable.

We are a hands-on team. We listen carefully, plan meticulously and style every detail ourselves, from the first sketch of a table plan to the final candle being lit. And because fresh flowers, decor, hiring and catering all sit under one roof, your day flows seamlessly, with one trusted partner looking after it all.

  • Personal & warmYou work directly with the people who bring your day to life.
  • Reliable & provenOver 850 events delivered on time, on brief and on budget.
  • Timeless styleRefined palettes and quality finishes that photograph beautifully.

What We Do

Decor, styling and catering for every occasion

Whatever you are celebrating, we design the setting, arrange the flowers, dress the space and take care of the food, so you can be fully present with the people you love. Decor and equipment hire is available for every occasion.

  • Weddings

    Romantic, elegant styling for ceremonies and receptions, from draping and florals to tablescapes, lighting and the finest finishing details.

  • Kiddies Parties

    Playful themes brought to life with colour, character and care. Balloon styling, themed tables and treats little guests will never forget.

  • Matric Balls

    Sophisticated, show-stopping decor for the night of a lifetime. Grand entrances, statement centrepieces and photo-ready moments.

  • Confirmations

    Graceful, understated styling for meaningful family milestones, with soft palettes, fresh florals and beautifully catered tables.

  • Baby Showers

    Gentle, dreamy settings to welcome a new arrival. Pastel palettes, sweet tables and thoughtful touches designed around your theme.

  • Bridal Showers

    Chic and celebratory styling for the bride-to-be, from garden brunches to elegant high teas, complete with delicious catering.

  • Birthday Celebrations

    Milestone birthdays, intimate dinners and joyful garden parties styled to reflect the guest of honour, at any age.

  • Corporate Events

    Polished, on-brand experiences for year-end functions, launches, conferences and client dinners, delivered with total professionalism.

  • Catering Services

    Seasonal menus prepared with care: canapés, plated dinners, harvest tables, sweet treats and everything in between.

Catering

Food that is as beautiful as the setting

Great celebrations are remembered for how they tasted as much as how they looked. Our kitchen team prepares fresh, seasonal menus that complement your styling, your budget and the way you want your guests to gather.

  • Canapés & Welcome Drinks

    Elegant bites and refreshing drinks to greet your guests on arrival.

  • Plated & Buffet Dinners

    Classic, generous menus served with grace, from two courses to five.

  • Harvest & Grazing Tables

    Abundant, styled spreads of cheeses, charcuterie, breads and fruit.

  • High Teas & Sweet Tables

    Delicate pastries, cakes and treats for showers, teas and birthdays.

  • Kiddies Party Platters

    Fun, wholesome party food that children love and parents approve of.

Halaal-friendly, vegetarian and dietary-specific options are available on request.

How It Works

From first conversation to the final toast

A calm, well-organised process is the secret to a relaxed celebration. Here is how we work with you.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    We meet in person or online to understand your occasion, your guests, your style and your budget.

  2. 02

    Concept & Quote

    You receive a tailored concept with colour palette, decor elements, menu suggestions and a transparent quotation.

  3. 03

    Planning & Preparation

    We confirm every detail, coordinate with your venue and suppliers, and prepare your decor and menu with care.

  4. 04

    Celebration

    On the day we set up, serve and pack down, so all you need to do is enjoy every moment with your guests.
