About Creations

A family-owned decor company in Wellington

Creations is a family-owned decor and event styling company in Wellington, in the heart of the Cape Winelands. Founded by Leandie O’Brien, we have spent more than a decade helping families, couples, schools and businesses celebrate the moments that matter most.

What began with fresh flowers and a passion for beautiful tables has grown into a complete celebration service: elegant wedding decor, thoughtful event styling, decor hire and delicious catering, all cared for by the same small team from the first conversation to the final toast.

We believe every celebration deserves warmth, care and a personal touch. That is why we listen closely, plan meticulously and style every detail ourselves, so that you can relax and simply enjoy your day with the people you love.

  • Family-owned & personalYou work directly with the people who bring your day to life.
  • Over a decade of trustHundreds of celebrations delivered on time, on brief and on budget.
  • Timeless, elegant styleRefined palettes and quality finishes that photograph beautifully.

Proudly Local

Your decor company in Wellington & the Western Cape

Creations is based in Wellington, in the heart of the Cape Winelands, and has been the local choice for wedding decor, party decor, matric ball decor and catering services for more than ten years. Because we are local, we know the region’s wine farms, churches, school halls and function venues, and we work closely with the florists, hire companies and venue teams you are likely to use.

We style celebrations throughout Wellington, Paarl, Stellenbosch, Franschhoek and Worcester, and we travel further across the Western Cape by arrangement. Whether you need full wedding styling or a single beautifully dressed table, request a quote and we will be in touch within one working day.
