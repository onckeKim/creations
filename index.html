About Creations

A family-owned studio celebrating life’s important milestones

Creations is a family-owned event styling business rooted in Wellington, in the heart of the Cape Winelands. Founded by Leandie O’Brien, we have spent more than a decade helping families, couples, schools and businesses celebrate the moments that matter most.

What began with fresh flowers and a passion for beautiful tables has grown into a complete celebration service: elegant decor, thoughtful styling, decor hire and delicious catering, all cared for by the same small team from the first conversation to the final toast.

We believe every celebration deserves warmth, care and a personal touch. That is why we listen closely, plan meticulously and style every detail ourselves, so that you can relax and simply enjoy your day with the people you love.

  • Family-owned & personalYou work directly with the people who bring your day to life.
  • Over a decade of trustHundreds of celebrations delivered on time, on brief and on budget.
  • Timeless, elegant styleRefined palettes and quality finishes that photograph beautifully.
