Our Story

Rooted in Wellington, built on trust

Creations began more than a decade ago with a simple belief: every celebration, however large or small, deserves to feel extraordinary. Founded by Leandie O’Brien in Wellington, in the heart of the Cape Winelands, we started with fresh flowers and a love of beautifully dressed tables.

Word travelled the way it does in a close community: one wedding led to a matric ball, one kiddies party to a corporate year-end function. As families returned to us for each new milestone, our services grew to include full wedding styling, event styling, decor hire and catering, so that one trusted team could care for the whole day.

Today we remain proudly family-owned and hands-on. The people you meet at your first consultation are the same people who arrange your flowers, set your tables and make sure every candle is lit before your guests arrive. That personal touch is the heart of Creations, and it always will be.

Our Mission

To turn life’s important moments into beautiful, effortless celebrations, styled with care, delivered with precision and remembered for a lifetime.

We believe that great events are built on trust. Our promise to every client is the same: we listen closely, we plan meticulously, we create with imagination, and we deliver exactly what we said we would, on time and to the highest standard.

  • Personal Service

Our Process

From first conversation to the final toast

A calm, well-organised process is the secret to a relaxed celebration. Here is how we work with you.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    We meet in person or online to understand your occasion, your guests, your style and your budget, and to answer every question you have.

  2. 02

    Concept & Quote

    You receive a tailored concept with colour palette, decor elements, floral and menu suggestions, and a clear, itemised quotation.

  3. 03

    Planning & Preparation

    We confirm every detail, coordinate with your venue and suppliers, source and prepare your decor, and finalise your menu with care.

  4. 04

    Celebration

    On the day we set up, style, serve and pack down, so all you need to do is enjoy every moment with the people you love.

Proudly Local

A local decor company in Wellington and the Cape Winelands

Creations is based in Wellington, a town we love for its mountain views, historic wine farms and warm community. Being local means we know the venues, the churches, the school halls and the farm barns of the region, and we have working relationships with the florists, hire companies and venues you are likely to use.

We regularly provide wedding decor, event styling, party decor and catering in Paarl, Stellenbosch, Franschhoek, Worcester and across the Winelands, and we are happy to travel further afield in the Western Cape by arrangement. See recent work in our gallery or request a quote.
